# Rules are recomputed on events and timers, with checks every second as a fallback

A Rule is worked out again when something may have changed its Level, not every second: when an entity in its conditions changes, when the time of a condition with a duration ("for 2 h") runs out (a timer set to 1 µs after the bound), at the bounds of a time condition, at midnight and whenever the home's time zone changes its UTC offset (summer time) or is replaced, and when what a template reads changes (Home Assistant's template tracking, as for its own template entities). Checking every second was what Home Assistant's own condition subscription does, but it recomputed the whole Rule for one timed condition, around the clock, and no long-lived logic in Home Assistant works that way; its triggers with `for:` use timers. The trade-offs we accept: a template that reads the time (`now()`) is recomputed once a minute, on second 0, so a template that compares seconds can change Level up to 59 s later than before; a template that reads all states is recomputed at most once a second (instead of Home Assistant's 60 s), which is no more often than before; and a template that reads something Home Assistant does not track, such as `area_entities()` or `random`, is only recomputed when something it tracks changes. Conditions neither Home Assistant nor AttentionSpan can watch (sun, moon, `device`, zone, `numeric_state` with `value_template`, a time read from an entity, a duration that is a template, and unknown conditions from integrations) are still checked every second. So is a condition with a duration while its start is unconfirmed, that is, set at the first check without seeing the change, until Home Assistant confirms it or the time has passed, because Home Assistant may know it began earlier but only answers yes or no. The start stays unconfirmed until the condition stops holding, so that a longer duration on the same condition is also checked until its own time has passed, and it stays unconfirmed when the entry is reloaded, until Home Assistant restarts and its own times count from the restart.
